Dataset:¶

The analysis uses netflix_data.csv from this repository. release_year refers to a title's release year, while date_added refers to when it was added to Netflix. The dataset includes movies and TV shows; the analysis below filters to movies released from 1990 through 1999. Counts describe titles in this dataset, not all movies released worldwide.

The most frequent movie duration among Netflix movies released in the 1990s was 94 minutes, appearing in 7 titles.

8) Q5 — Were any content gaps identified?

For this case study, define less represented as a genre with fewer titles than the median count across observed genre labels. This is a descriptive threshold, not evidence of unmet audience demand.

In [151]:
# Count movies per genre
genre_counts = movies_90_df["genre"].value_counts()

# Calculate mean and median
mean_count = genre_counts.mean()
median_count = genre_counts.median()

# Identify genres below the median
less_represented = genre_counts[
    genre_counts < median_count
]

print(f"Mean number of titles per genre: {mean_count:.1f}")
print(f"Median number of titles per genre: {median_count:.1f}")

9) Findings & Next Steps

Summary of Insights¶

This exploratory analysis examined Netflix movies released during the 1990s, focusing on movie duration, release years, and genre representation.

  • Most frequent runtime: 94 minutes was the most common movie duration, appearing in 7 titles.
  • Short Action movies: Only 7 Action movies were shorter than 90 minutes, representing approximately 3.8% of the 183 movies analyzed.
  • Peak release years: 1997, 1998, and 1999 had the highest number of movie releases, with 26 titles each.
  • Top genres: Action (48), Drama (44), and Comedy (40) accounted for approximately 72.1% of the movies analyzed.
  • Potential content gaps: Five genres fell below the median genre count of 11.5 movies: Stand-Up (8), Thrillers (5), Horror Movies (4), Documentaries (2), and Cult Movies (2).

Potential Applications¶

The findings suggest several opportunities for organizing and exploring the 1990s Netflix catalog:

  • Duration-Based Collections: Create collections of approximately 90-minute movies and short Action films to help viewers discover titles based on their available viewing time.
  • Nostalgic Curation: Highlight movies released between 1997 and 1999 through themed collections featuring late-1990s titles.
  • Genre Discovery: Feature Action, Drama, and Comedy in 1990s collections while creating dedicated sections for less-represented genres to improve their visibility.

Limitations & Next Steps¶

This analysis focuses on movies available in the Netflix dataset and does not represent all movies released worldwide during the 1990s.

The findings describe patterns in movie duration, release years, and genre representation within the analyzed catalog.

Future analysis could incorporate audience ratings and viewing trends to explore movie popularity and viewer preferences.
